## 📥 How to Download & Install

1. **Download the APK**
   - Click **[Download Latest APK](https://github.com/AIwolfie/Reelix-Releases/releases/latest)** to get the newest `Reelix-v*.apk`.
2. **Install on Android**
   - Open your device's notification or **Downloads** folder and tap the downloaded `.apk` file.
   - If prompted by Android, enable **Allow from this source** to grant permission for sideloading.
3. **Launch & Stream**
   - Open Reelix. Future updates will be notified automatically inside the app with seamless one-tap installs.

## ✨ Features

- 🚀 **Adaptive Streaming Engine**: Native multi-stream playback supporting DASH manifests, HLS streams, and direct MP4 with multi-resolution switching (4K, 1080p, 720p, 480p).
- 🔊 **Dialogue & Sound Booster**: Proprietary audio gain stage that boosts speech past your phone's default 100% volume ceiling — perfect for quiet dialogue and action mixes.
- 🍿 **Smart Binge Mode**: Auto-countdown for next episodes, cross-season transitions, and smart "Stay" controls that respect your viewing pace.
- ⚡ **Hardware Codec Auto-Negotiation**: Intelligently pairs your device with HEVC (H.265) or AVC (H.264) streams for optimal battery life and zero buffering.
- ⬇️ **Offline Media Downloads**: Download full movies and TV episodes with background download management, pause/resume, and local encrypted cache.
- 🖼️ **Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**: Keep watching your stream in a floating window while browsing or chatting.
- 🔄 **In-App Auto-Update System**: Integrated background update checker that notifies you and installs new versions directly without requiring Google Play Store.
- 🖤 **Cinematic OLED Dark UI**: Ultra-clean, modern fluid interface engineered with 60fps Flutter rendering.
